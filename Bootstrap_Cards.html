Lesson Notes

In this lesson, we learned how to use Bootstrap’s Card component. Cards are flexible content containers that can include images, text, buttons, lists, links, and more. They are perfect for product listings, profile boxes, blog previews, dashboards, and featured content.

Bootstrap Card Classes Used

  • card — the main wrapper that activates Bootstrap’s card styling.
  • card-img-top — places an image at the top of the card.
  • card-body — the section that holds text, titles, and buttons.
  • card-title — styles the card’s heading.
  • card-text — styles the paragraph text inside the card.
  • btn btn-secondary — a Bootstrap button styled with the “secondary” color.

Why Cards Are Useful

Cards are one of the most commonly used Bootstrap components because they are:

  • Responsive by default
  • Easy to customize
  • Perfect for layouts with repeated content
  • Great for mixing images, text, and actions

In future lessons, we can explore card groups, horizontal cards, colored cards, and cards with list groups.
